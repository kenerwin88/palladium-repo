THE DELIVERY CONTRACT

Boring in all the right ways.

  1. 01

    Prove every change

    Fast local checks become required CI checks. Preview the real artifact before merging.

  2. 02

    Build exactly once

    The same scanned container digest is promoted through development, staging, and production.

  3. 03

    Make recovery ordinary

    Small trunk merges, visible releases, and immutable versions make roll-forward routine.
